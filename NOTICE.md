(https://github.com/monarch-initiative/phenopacket-store; Danis et al., HGG Adv 2025;6:100371;

`THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/phenopacket-store-LICENSE`. The phenopackets were curated from published case reports.

their checksums are in `data/INPUT_CHECKSUMS.txt`.
